Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

Application: VitalTrack Pro

Important Medical Disclaimer: VitalTrack Pro is designed for personal fitness and health metrics tracking purposes only. It is not a certified medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified physician or healthcare provider for medical decisions.

1. Overview

VitalTrack Pro respects your privacy. We are committed to protecting your personal information and health data. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is collected, stored, and managed when using the VitalTrack Pro Android application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

VitalTrack Pro allows you to log and monitor personal health metrics including:

  • Blood Sugar (Glucose) levels
  • Blood Pressure (Systolic / Diastolic & Pulse)
  • Body Weight and Height (for BMI computation)
  • Water Intake logs
  • Heart Rate (BPM & SpO₂)
  • Medication schedules and reminders
  • Emergency Contact and Profile information

3. Local Storage and Data Security

Your health metrics and personal profile data are stored locally on your device using an encrypted local SQLite database (Android Room Framework). Your data is not uploaded to external servers or sold to third-party advertisers.

4. Data Sharing & Exports

You have complete ownership of your data. VitalTrack Pro allows you to export your health reports in CSV or PDF formats strictly for sharing with your personal healthcare provider at your sole discretion. We do not automatically share your health data with any third party.

5. App Permissions

VitalTrack Pro requests minimal Android device permissions necessary for app features:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to send timely medication and health reminder alerts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used strictly to trigger accurate alarm notifications for scheduled medications.
  • VIBRATE: Used to provide tactile feedback for active reminder alerts.

6. Data Deletion & Control

You maintain full control over your stored data. You can edit or permanently delete any log entry or clear your entire profile at any time directly within the app.
